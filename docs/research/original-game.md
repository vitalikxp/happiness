# Research: *The Pursuit of Happiness* (original board game)

**Reference only.** Use to understand mechanics and known design issues. Do **not** transcribe card
names, texts or numbers into our content (see `AGENTS.md` hard rule 1).

- Designers: David Chircop, Adrian Abela; development: Vangelis Bagiartakis. Artipia Games, 2015
  (Stronghold Games in US). RU edition «В погоне за счастьем» — «Низа Гамс», 2025.
- 1–4 players, 60–90 min. Expansions: Community (5th player, community cards), Experiences, Nostalgia
  (double-sided reminiscence cards), KS promos (Events, Trends, Pets, standalone Jobs).

## Components (base)
Board; 60 Projects, 60 Items & Activities, 18 Jobs, 12 double-sided Partners (+cover card), 9 Life Goals,
8 Child Traits; tokens for Knowledge/Creativity/Influence (36 each), 44 coins; hourglass markers
(9 per color, start with 6); tracks: Stress, Short-Term Happiness (STH), Long-Term Happiness (LTH), Round.

## Flow
- Rounds = life periods: Teen (round 1, no Jobs/Partners/Overtime), Adult rounds, Old Age from round 6.
  Max ~8 rounds (one review: "maximum of 8 rounds, but no minimum").
- **Upkeep:** advance round; discard & refill market (Projects and Items: 3 for 2–3p, 4 for 4p;
  Jobs & Partners = #players; Partners drawn from deck bottom); stress-zone hourglass adjustment;
  +1 stress per slot beyond 3 (Jobs/Partners count) and for an extra Partner; pay upkeep of Items/Job/
  Partners (any order, chaining allowed); unpaid ⇒ discard, +1 stress, −1 STH.
- **Action:** clockwise placement of one hourglass at a time on board space or own card; repeating a
  board space you already occupy costs 1 stress.
- **End of round:** group-project bonuses; set aside single-round/group projects and activities; return
  hourglasses; first player = highest STH; STH resets; stress stays.

## Actions
Study/Play/Interact (+3 Knowledge/Creativity/Influence), Temp Job (+3 money), Take Project, Spend
(item/activity, choose level), Get Job (one job max; market slot not refilled), Develop Relationship
(start at Date or advance one level if requirement met), Overtime (+2 hourglasses, +2 stress; not if it
would kill; removed in Old Age), Rest (−2 stress within current colour section), card actions
(advance Basic Project — may cost several hourglasses; Job promotion if next-level job of same type is
on the board — costs 1 hourglass to Spent Time + promotion cost; gives promotion reward incl. LTH, not
the new job's hire reward).

## Projects
Basic (4 levels, advance one at a time; completed L4 frees slot), Single-Round (choose any one level),
Group (4 roles; others join; max 2 roles/player; end-of-round bonus per role scaled by covered roles).
Category letters (Arts, Business, Culture, Health, Politics, Social, Tech&Science) are thematic only.

## Tracks
- **Stress:** coloured sections; entering a worse section ⇒ −1 hourglass per round; past the end ⇒ death.
  Reduce via Rest (−2), Relax (−1), both within section; Good Health (heart) moves one section left
  (same relative space) and +1 hourglass next upkeep. Old Age adds increasing stress each round
  (printed on round track); without health improvements the 2nd Old Age round is usually fatal;
  surviving into the 3rd ⇒ die at its end.
- **STH:** +/−; resets each round; highest goes first; each STH point = −1 resource on project advances
  (reviews: negative STH increases costs); overflow converts to LTH; spend 1 STH to refresh a market row.
- Discarding an own card any time: +1 stress, −1 STH.

## End & scoring
All dead ⇒ inheritance (+1 LTH per 5 of a resource type / 5 money) + Life Goals (sole best player only;
ties ⇒ nobody). Most LTH wins. **Solo:** 3 Life Goals with solo conditions; win = all 3 + ≥ 50 LTH.

## Critical reception (summary)
- Praise: theme–mechanics fit, story-telling at the table, replayability via card variety, approachable
  worker placement, friendly interaction, decent solo.
- Criticism: opaque resource→LTH conversion; rare Good-Health cards ⇒ perceived luck; information
  density; dense rulebook; downtime at 4p; random market can restrict strategy; light for heavy gamers.
- No consensus dominant strategy (job rush strong but not overwhelming; jobless players can compete).
