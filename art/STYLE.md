# Art style guide — «книжная миниатюра»

Card illustrations for the `fantasy` pack. The game works without them (`docs/CONTENT.md §1`): a card
without a picture keeps its text-only look with the illuminated initial. Pictures are AI-generated by
the owner from the prompts in [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md), curated by hand and credited in
[CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md).

## 1. Look

A page from a warm medieval chronicle, the way the UI already looks:

- **Medium:** egg tempera and shell gold on cream parchment; thin iron-gall ink outlines; flat
  colour fields with light modelling. No glossy digital painting, no photo or 3D look.
- **Perspective:** flat, "medieval" — stacked planes, buildings slightly tilted toward the viewer.
- **Palette:** the UI tokens (`src/ui/styles/main.css`) — parchment `#f2e6cc`, ink `#2b1d14`,
  cinnabar `#a3301b`, lapis `#1f4a85`, verdigris `#2c6a55`, ochre, gold `#c99a3a`. One or two of the
  bright colours dominate a picture; gold is an accent, not a background.
- **Mood:** cozy, gently ironic fairy tale (`docs/CONTENT.md §2`). Small jokes are welcome (the cat
  who owns the house, the polite polar bear); menace and gore are not.
- **People:** small, at a distance or from behind, no close-up faces. One picture must fit every hero
  and both versions of a partner, so partners are drawn through their things and places.

## 2. Composition by card kind

| Kind | What is drawn |
|---|---|
| Basic / single-round / group project | the deed in progress: a workplace, a site, an event |
| Item | the thing itself in its place |
| Activity | the pastime as a small scene |
| Job | the workplace and its tools |
| Partner | a still life of the partner's things in their surroundings |
| Child Trait | a cradle with the omen around it |
| Life Goal | an allegorical emblem |

The subject sits in the centre two thirds: the UI crops the picture to a wide band on market cards.

## 3. Prompts

Each prompt is the shared style template plus the card's subject; both live in
`src/content/packs/fantasy/art.ts` (`ART_STYLE`, `artSubjects`). Edit them there and run `pnpm art`
to regenerate [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md). Use the shared negative prompt where the tool supports one.

Never put the original game's name, card titles or artists in a prompt, and never prompt "in the
style of" a living artist.

## 4. Delivery

- Generate at 4:3, at least 1024×768. Pick the best of several takes; regenerate rather than
  retouch heavily.
- Export **640×480 WebP**, quality ~80, at most 120 KB (`pnpm art` warns about heavier files).
- Name the file by card id: `art/cards/fantasy.project.forge_blade.webp`. The build picks up
  every file found there; no code change is needed.
- Add a row to [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md): tool and model, prompt (or "as in PROMPTS.md"), date,
  license terms of the tool.
- Run `pnpm art`: it marks the done cards in PROMPTS.md and fails on unknown ids or heavy files.

## 5. Review checklist

- Reads at 240 px wide; the subject is clear without the card name.
- No letters, pseudo-letters or signatures anywhere (models love to add them — crop or regenerate).
- Palette and line match the other pictures in the same row of the market.
- Nothing from the original game: no recognisable compositions of its cards.
